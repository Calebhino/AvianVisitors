# Avian Visitors v1.3.1

Birds of America brings 439 identified species and 819 illustrations to [the bundle catalog](https://avianvisitors.com/bundles). Audubon's birds retain the flowers, branches and overlapping compositions from the original plates. Open a postcard to choose among the available illustrations; your choice is remembered in that browser.

Update your station with **Tools > Pull latest**, then choose **Birds of America** in **Settings > Bird bundle**, or run:

```sh
sudo avian-bundle use official-birds-of-america
```

A station with a saved location downloads its local species and all their illustration choices. The full collection uses about 753 MB of unique optimized PNGs. The catalog's 782 MiB estimate includes references shared between species. Existing downloaded artwork stays selected through the software update.

This update adds numbered illustration choices to the station and BirdFrame readers while retaining existing bundle compatibility, exact image checksums, bounded image validation and atomic installation. Earlier releases need this reader update before installing Birds of America.

Artwork: John James Audubon; engravers including Robert Havell and William Home Lizars. Source: the Darlington Collection, Archives & Special Collections, University of Pittsburgh Library System. The library's **No Copyright — United States** statement is preserved as a rights statement, not a worldwide public-domain license. [Source credits, per-image provenance and rights](https://avianvisitors.com/bundles/birds-of-america).

Station PNGs use ordinary transparency-aware resizing. All 819 were compared with their full-resolution originals at 560- and 1,024-pixel display sizes on light and dark backgrounds; selected worst-difference cases were also visually inspected. The full collection passed a local install, checksum verification and silhouette generation for all 883 species/illustration choices. This is software and image verification, not a new physical-panel test.
